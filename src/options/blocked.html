Marginalia can't run on this page

Your browser's policy or this extension's site-access setting is blocking its content script.

Most likely causes

  • Site access — open chrome://extensions (or your browser's add-ons page), find Marginalia, and set Site access to On all sites or add this site explicitly.
  • Managed profile policy — an admin may block extensions on this domain via runtime_blocked_hosts. Check chrome://policy and ask your administrator about the ExtensionSettings policy.
  • Firefox quarantined domains — Mozilla may quarantine extensions on certain domains; verify the add-on's permissions in about:addons.

Once access is allowed, reload the page — the margin comment box appears when you select text in an AI answer.
