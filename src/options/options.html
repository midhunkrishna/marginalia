Research mode

A research turn runs a multi-step loop: the provider ticked Use for research above searches the web and can read pages, with that provider's key. Research uses many more tokens per answer than a normal follow-up, so it costs more — check your provider's pricing (the Pricing links above). Besides the AI provider, your query goes to the search engine below, and the pages the model reads are fetched by the extension.

Grants the extension permission to fetch any web page, only used while a research turn runs. Your browser will ask you to confirm.

Quick actions

One-tap chips above the box on a new thread. Tapping a chip sends its prompt right away about the highlighted text (Shift-click to edit it first). Writing {{selection}} in a prompt is optional — the highlight is always the anchor.
